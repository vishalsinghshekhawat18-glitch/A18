⏰ Status: implemented — approved via supplementary demands + Appropriation Bill 2026.

📰 **Income Tax Rules 2026** *(standing exception — major reform)*
- Effective **April 1, 2026**; implements Income-tax Act 2025; Sections **819→536**; Chapters 47→23; Words 5.12 lakh→2.6 lakh
- **A) Compliance:** dividends payable only within India; 7-year audit-trail retention for exchanges; zero-coupon bond pre-approval (3 months, 2-agency investment-grade rating)
- **B) Digital/cross-border tax:** significant-economic-presence threshold = ₹2 crore transaction OR 3 lakh users
- **C) HRA:** metro-city 50% exemption expanded **4→8 cities** (Mumbai, Kolkata, Delhi, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru)
- **D) PAN thresholds:** cash deposit/withdrawal ₹50,000/day → **₹10 lakh/year**; vehicle >₹5 lakh; hotel/event >₹1 lakh (was ₹50,000); property >₹20 lakh (was ₹10 lakh)
- **E) Credit cards:** annual spend >₹10 lakh reportable; PAN linkage mandatory to issue
🧠 Mnemonic → "819→536, 47→23, 4 metros→8 metros"
🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — Don't confuse HRA city count (4→8) with PAN cash threshold (₹50,000/day→₹10 lakh/year).
💼 Interview Q → *Significance of Income Tax Rules 2026?* → Operationalise the Income-tax Act 2025 — simpler, shorter law with tighter PAN-linked compliance on high-value transactions.
⏰ Status: implemented as scheduled April 1, 2026 — no reversal found.

📰 **Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (Amendment) Bill/Act, 2025-26**
- Passed **Lok Sabha 30 March 2026**; Select Committee (Chair: Baijayant Panda), report 17 Dec 2025
- **A) CIIRP** (new Chapter IV-A): creditor-led route, ≥51% financial-creditor approval, timeline **150 days + 45-day extension**, promoters stay under RP oversight
- **B) Faster timelines:** 14 days to admit/reject (Sec 7/9/10); 30 days to approve resolution plans
- **C) CIRP revival:** once, within 120 days, before liquidation (previously none)
- **D) CoC:** can replace liquidator with 66% vote
- **E) Liquidation:** must complete in **180 days (+90-day extension)**; secured creditors act within 14 days or lose rights
- **F) Penalties:** frivolous cases ₹1 lakh–₹2 crore; non-compliance up to ₹5 crore or 3× gains/loss
🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — CIIRP (150+45 days) vs Liquidation (180+90 days) confusion pair.
⏰ Status: implemented — passed Rajya Sabha 1 April 2026, Presidential assent 6 April 2026 → **IBC (Amendment) Act, 2026 (Act No. 6 of 2026)**; provisions notified separately.
