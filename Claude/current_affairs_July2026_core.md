📰 **EPFO Overhaul** (merged — 3 linked reforms)
🪝 EPFO rewrote its rulebook this month: a new 2026 scheme, faster settlements, and a modernised IT backbone.
- **A. EPF Scheme 2026** (replaces 1952 scheme): "principal employer" concept introduced for contract workers for the first time; employer contribution due within 15 days of month-end; contribution linked to ₹15,000 wage ceiling; rate unchanged at 12% (10% for notified establishments)
- **B. 3-day settlement mandate:** PF claims settle in **3 days**; pension/EDLI claims in **20 days**; 12% penal interest on delays beyond 20 days; full-withdrawal age lowered to **55 years**
- **C. CITES 2.01 platform:** migrates ~340 million accounts to one national system; auto-settlement limit ₹1 lakh → **₹5 lakh** for KYC-compliant members; FY26 interest credited by July 15 (vs usual Oct–Nov)
🎯 Exam Angle → 🔥 HIGH — Multi-fact trap: 3 days vs 20 days vs 55 years vs ₹15,000 vs ₹5 lakh.
💼 Interview Q → *"What changed for contract workers under EPF 2026?"* → Introduces "principal employer" liability for the first time, closing a compliance gap that let contract labour fall outside EPF coverage.
