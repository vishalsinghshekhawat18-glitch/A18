NOTE 01  ·  01 AUG 2026  ·  SECTION 1 (ESI & FINANCE)

India crosses 300 GW non-fossil fuel capacity; renewables push continues

  • India hit 300.50 GW of installed non-fossil fuel electricity capacity (as of Jul 31, 2026) — 60%+ of its 500 GW-by-2030 target, already 54%+ of total installed capacity (552 GW).
  • Break-up: Solar 164.59 GW | Wind 58.14 GW | Hydro 57.24 GW | Bio-power 11.75 GW | Nuclear 8.78 GW
  • 2025-26 addition: 55.29 GW (Solar 44.6 + Wind 6) | Renewable generation: 477.79 BU (2025-26), up from 190.96 BU (2014-15)
  • Brookfield launches "Lumara" — dedicated India renewable platform, ~$600 mn deployment (solar/wind/BESS, 6+ GW initial); funded via Brookfield's Catalytic Transition Fund (anchor: UAE's ALTÉRRA, up to $5 bn corpus). Brookfield's total India wind+solar portfolio: ~45 GW; total India investment >$32 bn. Nawal Saini — Managing Partner & Head of Energy (South Asia & ME). HQ Brookfield: New York/Toronto; CEO Bruce Flatt.
🎯 Exam Angle: Don't confuse: 300.50 GW (national non-fossil capacity) vs Brookfield's $600 mn Lumara deployment vs Brookfield's $32 bn total India investment — three different numbers, same theme.
