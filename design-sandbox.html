PM Electric Drive Revolution in Innovative Vehicle Enhancement (PM E-DRIVE) Central Sector Scheme
🏛️ Nodal Ministry Ministry of Heavy Industries (MHI)
📅 Launch Year & Duration Launched Oct 2024 · Extended to 31 Mar 2028
💰 Total Budget Outlay ₹11,900 Crore (100% Central Funding)
🎯 Target & Segments 24.79 Lakh e2Ws · 3.16 Lakh e3Ws · 14,028 e-Buses · 88,500 EV Fast Chargers
📜 Past Lineage & Predecessor:
Replaced the older FAME-II Scheme (Faster Adoption and Manufacturing of Electric Vehicles, ₹11,500 Cr, ended March 2024). Unlike FAME-II, PM E-DRIVE introduced Aadhaar-authenticated e-vouchers for buyers and shifted focus heavily toward commercial e-buses and public charging stations.
📈 Current 2026 Milestone in News:
The government extended the scheme tenure by 1 year to March 31, 2028 while tapering down the e2W subsidy from ₹5,000/kWh to ₹2,500/kWh (max ₹5,000 per vehicle) for electric 2-wheelers with ex-factory price ≤ ₹1.5 lakh.
🎯 Exam Trap (SBI PO / RBI Grade B): Total outlay remains fixed at ₹11,900 Cr (not increased); hybrid cars are excluded (only pure EVs eligible); subsidy per e2W was halved from ₹10,000 (FY25) to ₹5,000 (FY26).

Govt halves e2W subsidy, extends PM E-DRIVE to March 2028

  • PM E-DRIVE extended 1 yr to 31 Mar 2028; e2W incentive cut ₹5,000/kWh → ₹2,500/kWh; max incentive per e2W now ₹5,000 (was ₹10,000 FY25); eligible e2Ws: ex-factory price up to ₹1.5 lakh; total outlay unchanged: ₹11,900 cr
🎯 Exam Angle: Extension date (Mar 2028) + subsidy halved — before/after trap.

📜 Past Lineage & Predecessor:
Replaced the earlier Grid-Connected Rooftop Solar Programme Phase-II (which had a modest 4 GW target). PM Surya Ghar overhauled the subsidy delivery mechanism by providing direct DBT cash subsidies into bank accounts (₹30,000 for 1kW, ₹60,000 for 2kW, max ₹78,000 for 3kW+) plus collateral-free loans at ~7% interest from public sector banks.

🎯 Exam Trap (SBI PO / RBI Grade B): Maximum central financial assistance (subsidy) caps at ₹78,000 for 3 kW systems (no extra subsidy beyond 3 kW for individual residential homes); nodal financing agency is REC Limited.
