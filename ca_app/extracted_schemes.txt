Approved for Central Government employees, guarantees assured pension, family pension, and
minimum pension benefits for 23 lakh employees. Key features include:
 Assured Pension: 50% of the average basic pay over the last 12 months, with proportionate

 Assured Family Pension: 60% of the employee's pension to the family after death.

superannuation.

 Duration: 2 years.

 Objective: To promote job creation in the manufacturing sector.

 Target: 30 lakh beneficiaries.
 Duration: 2 years.
o Scheme C:
 Objective: To support employers in hiring new employees.
 Details: Employers will receive a monthly incentive of Rs 3,000 for appointing new

 Target: 50 lakh youth beneficiaries.
o Scheme D:

 This scheme aims to enhance skill development across India, with a focus on the youth,

 Type: Centrally Sponsored.
 Target: Skill training for 20 lakh youth over 5 years.
