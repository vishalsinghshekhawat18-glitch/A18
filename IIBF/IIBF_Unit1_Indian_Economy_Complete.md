India Population

Rectangle 7

As per Ministry of Statistics and Program Implementation (report dated 9th Dec.2021) The current population of India is 1,413,210,712 as of December 29, 2022, based on interpolation of the latest United Nations data. India is the second most populous country on the earth, next to China has 17.7 per cent of world population, with meagre 2.4 per cent of the world surface area India likely to overtake China’s population by year 2027 India is at stage three of the four- stage model of demographic transition - moved from low population growth rate with high birth and mortality rates (in the pre industrialization stage), to stable population with decrease in birth rate and low death rate, as a result of improved economic conditions rising population imposes greater economic burden on the nation the society therefore has to make a much greater effort to initiate the process of growth
